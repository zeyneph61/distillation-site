Tray-Type Distillation Column — Sieve-Tray Unit for Corn Ethanol Plants

Continuous distillation Sieve tray AISI 304 SS Ethanol

A continuous sieve-tray distillation column designed to process corn-mash feed and deliver 100,000 L/day of 95.6 % ethanol distillate. Column body in AISI 304 stainless steel; 70 sieve trays; condenser, reboiler and reflux drum included.

This sieve-tray distillation column was designed as part of FDE 450 Process Design at Ankara University, Department of Food Engineering. The system applies McCabe–Thiele stage analysis at a reflux ratio of 4.30 to separate an ethanol–water mixture derived from corn-mash fermentation.

The column processes feed continuously through 70 AISI 304 stainless-steel sieve trays across a 28.8 m column height (Ø 2.311 m). The auxiliary circuit consists of a horizontal shell-and-tube condenser, a vertical thermosyphon reboiler, and a horizontal reflux drum. All hydraulic parameters were verified against vapor/liquid flood limits.

Equipment & Components

Distillation column (tray)
AISI 304 · Ø 2.311 m · H 28.8 m
Tray distillation column is selected as distillation column. Tray columns have high separation efficiency, so they are widely used in industrial systems.
Sieve trays
AISI 304 · 70 trays · 0.5" holes
Sieve trays were chosen as the tray system. They are less expensive and simpler in structure than valve and bubble-cap trays, and are widely used in large-scale ethanol distillation.
Condenser
Horizontal shell & tube · H 1.8 m
A horizontal shell-and-tube condenser was selected. Shell-and-tube condensers have high temperature and pressure resistance and a large heat-transfer area, and are widely used in industry.
Reboiler
Vertical thermosyphon · H 1.2 m
A thermosyphon reboiler was chosen to provide evaporation at the bottom of the column. It does not require an additional pump, which lowers operating costs.
Reflux drum
Horizontal · L 3.6 m
A horizontal reflux drum was selected. It is preferred because it provides a larger vapor–liquid separation area and maintains a stable liquid level.
